# Thorsten Keim

> ***Engineer. Technology Leader. AI Architect. Creator & Architect of DHINX.***

Thorsten Keim is an **engineer, technology leader and AI architect** with **more than 30 years of professional experience** spanning business processes, enterprise systems, quality management, data, analytics and digital transformation. For more than three years, he has been intensively exploring the potential of **generative AI and large language models**, with a particular focus on how AI can transform software architectures, processes, human interaction and increasingly **autonomous digital systems**.

He holds a degree in **Electrical Engineering and Information Technology** from TH Mittelhessen University of Applied Sciences and began his professional career in the **automotive industry in 1995**.

Over the following decades, he progressed from operational and consulting roles into **international program and leadership positions**. He has led global initiatives in **process harmonization, enterprise applications, digital solutions, business intelligence and analytics**, consistently working at the intersection of business, processes, technology and data.

His experience includes **leading multidisciplinary teams and major transformation programs** across global organizations — from standardized digital information exchange and enterprise-wide process harmonization to the integration of heterogeneous ERP and data landscapes and the development of global analytics and management solutions.

Today, Thorsten serves as **Head of Quality Business Performance, Analytics & Solutions at AUMOVIO**. Independently of his professional role, he is the **Founder and Architect of DHINX**, an independently conceived initiative whose origins date back to 2015.

> ***Engineer by education. Transformation leader by experience. AI architect by passion. Founder by conviction.***
